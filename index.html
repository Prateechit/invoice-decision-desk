      The process, stage by stage

      Approved
      Every hard check passes. Scheduled on the vendor's terms to the account on file.
      Needs review
      Fraud signals, PO/receipt mismatches, an unknown vendor or a big amount. Goes to a named owner with a recommendation.
      Returned to vendor
      The invoice itself is defective (missing fields, bad maths). The vendor gets a drafted email saying exactly what to fix.
      Rejected
      Duplicate, or the vendor is blocked. Closed with the evidence attached.
